# Joelib
A projection of the Joe universe to the human universe, with files describing a mysterious Joe to be simultaneously a language, all physical units and quantities, all chemical elements and everything else.

## 1. Overview & Introduction
The Joe Wiki, see the Wiki page of this repository.

## 2. Literature, Language, Culture
- **Joeglish Grammar** discovered by Joe Jin

    A study on the Joeglish Grammar, an attempt to understand the Joe language.
  
    [Joeglish Grammar Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Rx4SeMx0YsXyF8olZoW1U-QVDSS87hqsPKRqKvyWZwk/edit?tab=t.0)

- **Joegle Translate** by Wayne Xu

  After studying the Joe language, we made this Joeglish translator to better understand Joeglish, and help establish an effortless conversion between English and Joeglish.
  
  [Joegle Translate Website](https://translate.united-taylors.org/)

- **The Great Joetsby - by F. Scott Fitzjoerald** discovered by Derek Li
  
  Literature of the Joe Universe discovered by Derek Li, a book called The Great Joetsby, one of the greatest literature of Joe universe.
  
  [The Great Joetsby Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pin7tftCd_qfugSu4F8u416EfudLGgKtUS5VQB3XRIg/edit?usp=sharing)

- **Joelet Speech [Complete]** discovered by Emilia Marcucci
  
  To Joe, or not to Joe, that is the question. Another great piece of Joe culture and literature.

  [Joelet Speech Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13pYh0LqbIk-Se-KqCLpDiuw-YEe-KBjcc0Ycrk2IGtA/edit?tab=t.0)

- **Collection of Joekemons** discovered by Matthew
  
  Joekemons are a set of creatures recorded in the Joe universe, whether they are true or not still remains unknown.
  
  [Collection of Joekemons Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1S_ddb_ZT8GrfKzUo3D6C8nZXgqY47dyfZkKU2SXYIM0/edit?gid=0#gid=0)
